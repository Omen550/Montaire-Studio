04 / Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on your project's size, design requirements, and features. Contact Montaire Studio with your idea to discuss an individual quote.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes! We can discuss refreshing the appearance, layout, and mobile experience of an existing website. The available options depend on your current platform.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is part of our approach. Websites are designed to adapt to phones, tablets, and desktops.

Do I need to live near Montaire Studio?

Not at all. Projects can be discussed, designed, and delivered remotely. We can work together online regardless of your location.

How do I get started?

Simply fill out the project inquiry form below. Tell us about your business and the type of website you're looking for so we can discuss the next steps.
